# Malmberg's Refund Policy (fictional)

1. Refunds are issued only to a customer whose identity was confirmed with `get_customer`, for an order that belongs to that customer.
2. Agents may refund up to $500 per order. Anything above $500 needs a human.
3. Perishable items that arrive warm, damaged, or spoiled: full refund or free replacement, customer's choice. Ask for a photo.
4. Late delivery of perishables (outside the delivery window): refund shipping, or full refund if the customer reports spoilage.
5. An order can be refunded once. Already-refunded orders cannot be refunded again.
6. Non-perishable items: refund within 30 days of delivery, unopened only.

This policy does not cover price matching, subscriptions, or wholesale accounts.

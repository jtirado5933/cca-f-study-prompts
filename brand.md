# Trace3 Full Throttle: Brand Reference (GTM / internal seller materials)

## Positioning
Trace3 is the premier provider of IT solutions and consultation services, delivering business transformation through elite engineering and dynamic innovation across data, security, cloud, and AI.

"Full Throttle" = energy, speed, focus, disciplined execution. Copy should feel like momentum: confident, action-first, never corporate filler.

Standard footer tagline: **All Possibilities Live In Technology**

## Typography
- Open Sans Light (300): subtitles, large soft text
- Open Sans Regular (400): body copy
- Open Sans Semibold (600): subheadings, labels
- Open Sans Bold (700/800): headings, emphasis, price
- Fallback: Arial Regular/Bold/Black

Suggested scale for US Letter: title 30-34pt Bold; subtitle 12-13pt Light; section heads 9.5-11pt Bold uppercase, letter-spaced, Trace3 Blue; body 9-10pt; footer 7-7.5pt.

## Motifs
- Network/particle fields: small circles (r 1-3px) joined by 0.5-1px lines, Trace3 Blue at 20-60% opacity.
- Polygons: low-poly triangles/hexagons outlined thinly.
- Speed lines: 3-6 thin parallel angled strokes, fading out.
- Angled section edges (clip-path polygon) and dark→Trace3 Blue gradient sweeps.
- Place motifs in header/footer bands and page margins, behind nothing that needs reading.

## Copy patterns (GTM)
- Section heads: "THE OFFER", "WHO TO CALL ON", "CUSTOMER OUTCOMES", "DISCOVERY QUESTIONS", "WHY TRACE3 WINS", "HANDLING OBJECTIONS", "GTM MOTION", "SELLER RESOURCES".
- Bullets start with a verb or an outcome noun. Discovery questions are open-ended and sound like something a seller would actually say out loud.
- Numbers: only if supplied by the user; otherwise `[METRIC]`.
- Written for Trace3 sellers, not the customer. Say "customers" or "the buyer," never address the reader as the client.
- No commercials anywhere: no pricing, rates, margins, discounts, deal sizes, quota or comp, and no SOW terms. If the user supplies any, leave it off the page and tell them why.
- Timeline wording: "Days 1-30", "Days 31-60", "Days 61-90", always with the note "Directional plan; adjust with the practice lead."
